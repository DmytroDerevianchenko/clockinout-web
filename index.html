Good tools shouldn't be priced per head

Tracking hours, paying people right and knowing what a job really costs are what keep a small business alive. Yet the software for it is usually billed per worker, per month, as if every crew were a corporation. ClockInOut is built on a simpler idea: the essentials should be within reach of a contractor with three guys and a truck.

It was built by a framing contractor, for his own crew first, and it runs that company's payroll and invoicing today. It's the same app you'd be using, tested on real sites, real paycheques and real clients.
