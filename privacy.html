Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 · Verified Works Inc.

ClockInOut ("the app", "we", "us") is a time-tracking, payroll and invoicing app for small crews, operated by Verified Works Inc., 405-2405 Lake Shore Blvd W, Etobicoke, Ontario M8V 1C6, Canada. An employer sets up an organization and invites workers; workers clock in and out of shifts. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what your choices are.

The short version

  • We collect your location only while you are clocked in to a shift, and your phone shows a visible notification the entire time it happens.
  • Location history is deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Your employer can see your time entries and, if they enabled location features, your clock-in and clock-out locations and shift route.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you off the clock.

What we collect

Account information. Name, email address, phone number, and job details (pay rate, role), provided by you or by the employer who invited you. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email from that provider; we never see your password there.

Time and attendance data. Clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, shifts, schedules, and notes attached to them. This is the core function of the app.

Location data. If your employer enables location features, the app records GPS positions only during an active shift, with a persistent visible notification. It is used for two things:

  • Geofence check: confirming you are within the work site your employer defined, to allow clock-in or clock-out or to flag time outside the site.
  • Shift record: periodic location points during the shift, visible to your employer as a route map.

When you clock out, or the shift is closed automatically, location collection stops. No location is collected while you are off the clock, and none is collected if your employer has location features turned off.

Payroll and business data. Hours, wages, deductions, payments and, for employers, clients and invoices. Entered by you or your employer.

Device data. A push-notification token so the app can send you shift and schedule alerts, and basic technical logs needed to run the service.

How long we keep it

  • GPS location points: 90 days, then permanently deleted by an automatic daily job.
  • Time, payroll and payment records: kept while the organization is active, because they are employment records your employer may be legally required to keep.
  • Account data: kept until the account is deleted (see Delete your account).

Who can see what

Your employer (the organization's admins) can see your time entries, pay calculations and, only if location features are on, your clock locations and shift routes. Workers cannot see each other's data. We access data only to run, support and fix the service.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app, and each receives only what its job requires: Render (application and database hosting), Google Cloud Storage (generated PDF and CSV files), Google Firebase and Apple Push Notification service (notifications), Brevo (transactional email such as invites, password resets and invoices), and Google and Apple sign-in (optional). We do not sell or rent personal information, and there is no advertising or third-party analytics in the app.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Sign-in uses signed tokens, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to production data is limited to the operator.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission is requested in the app before first use and can be turned off at any time in your phone's settings. If your employer requires on-site clock-in, turning it off may prevent clocking in through the app. That requirement is your employer's policy, not ours.
  • Access, correction and deletion: ask your employer's admin to correct employment records, or contact us at dmytro@clockinout.ca. We reply within 30 days. Employment records may be exempt from deletion where the law requires your employer to keep them.
  • We follow Canada's PIPEDA. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

ClockInOut is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
