ClockInOut by Verified Works Inc.

How to request deletion

Email dmytro@clockinout.ca from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my ClockInOut account". We confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you can't send from that address, tell us your name and your employer's organization and we will verify the request another way before deleting anything.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and profile: name, email, phone number, address and linked Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your location history.
  • Your device notification tokens.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Work time and pay records (hours, pay and payments) belong to your employer's payroll records. Employment and tax law usually requires employers to keep these, in Canada for at least 6 years. They are kept for your employer's organization and removed from your personal account.
  • If you are an employer deleting your organization, you can export all your records to CSV first. We then delete the organization and its data, except where the law requires us to keep something.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, within 30 days.
