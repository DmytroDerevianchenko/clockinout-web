Common questions

I'm a worker and I can't sign in.
Your employer invites you from the app. Open the invite email on your phone, or use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen with the email your employer used.

My hours look wrong.
Only your employer can edit time entries. Ask your admin to correct the shift; the change shows up in your hours and pay right away.

Why does the app want my location?
Only if your employer turned on job-site check-in. Location is recorded while you're clocked in, never off shift, and it's deleted after 90 days. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Can I get my data out?
Yes. Admins can export hours, payroll, payments and the team list to CSV from the statistics screens.

How do I delete my account?
See Delete your account.
